---
name: Customer Google Calendar invitations
description: Product boundary for inviting trip customers through Google Calendar.
---

Send one separate Google Calendar invitation per confirmed reservation on a published trip, using the agency's connected staff calendar. Keep customer attendees off the shared staff trip event, and do not add customer Google OAuth.

**Why:** the user selected individual email invitations and explicitly rejected customer OAuth; the shared trip event remains an agency/staff event.

**How to apply:** preserve this split when changing trip sync, reservation lifecycle hooks, calendar notifications, or invitation delivery.
